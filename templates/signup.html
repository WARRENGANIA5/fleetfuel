Terms & Conditions

This system is an internal tool of the {{ org.office_name }} ({{ org.office_short }}) of {{ org.org_name }}, provided for use by authorized {{ org.office_short }} and motorpool personnel only.

Privacy Policy

This system collects and stores information necessary for government fleet fuel management, including:

  • Account information (username, full name, email, profile photo).
  • Trip records, vehicle details, and fuel consumption data you enter.
  • Fuel consumption predictions generated by the system's Linear Regression model.

This data is used solely for fleet management, fuel request validation, and reporting within the {{ org.office_name }} of {{ org.org_name }}. It is not shared with third parties outside official government reporting requirements.

Passwords are stored using one-way cryptographic hashing and are never visible to administrators or stored in plain text.
